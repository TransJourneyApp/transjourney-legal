Transjourney Support

Need help with Transjourney? Contact us about app features, technical problems, privacy questions or Premium subscriptions.

Contact

Email Transjourney.support@gmail.com. Please describe the issue and, if relevant, your iOS version and app version.

For your privacy: Please do not email sensitive journal entries, health records, photos, voice recordings or backup files unless specifically necessary and you understand the risks.

Premium subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed through Apple. You can manage or cancel a subscription through your Apple Account. If Premium access is missing, try the app's Restore Purchases option.

Backups and data

Transjourney stores your records on your device. Backup and export files may contain sensitive information and are not encrypted by Transjourney. Keep copies somewhere you trust. Removing the app or deleting data does not cancel an Apple subscription.
